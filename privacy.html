Privacy Policy

Your data stays under your control.

Effective October 27, 2026

Data Inspector Pro has no analytics, third-party crash reporting, developer account, or telemetry. The app processes your files locally. Apple handles App Store purchases through StoreKit.

01

Data we collect

The developer does not collect your files or usage data. The app has no developer-operated server, analytics SDK, or third-party crash reporter. StoreKit communicates with Apple to handle purchases and subscription status.

The subscription is bought through the App Store. Apple takes the payment and provides the app with purchase and entitlement information. The developer does not receive your payment details. Apple handles that information under its own privacy policy.

02

The files you open

You choose every file yourself — through the open panel, a drag onto the window, or Finder's "Open With." The app reads that file to display its schema and rows and, if you unlock it, to write your edits back. The app does not upload the file or report its contents to the developer. Files stored in a synced folder may also be handled by the sync service you use.

03

Safety copies and snapshots

By default, when you first unlock a file for editing, Data Inspector Pro may copy it into the app's own sandboxed container so File ▸ Revert to Safety Copy can restore the original exactly. A copy is also taken before a change that cannot be undone, such as dropping a table. These automatic copies are stored locally and are eligible for cleanup after two weeks. You can change safety-copy settings, and size limits may apply.

Snapshots you take yourself, from File ▸ Snapshots, are kept until you delete them — deleting something you deliberately named and chose to keep would not be housekeeping. They live in the same container and are stored locally as well. Everything the app has copied, automatic or not, is listed with its size in Settings ▸ Behaviour and can be removed there at any time with Delete All.

04

Encrypted databases

Encrypted Realm and SQLCipher files are decrypted in memory so their contents can be shown. The key or passphrase you type is held in memory for as long as the file is open and is never written to disk, never stored in the keychain, and never sent anywhere. No decrypted copy of the file is written either — unless you ask for one with File ▸ Save Decrypted Copy, which writes a plain, unencrypted database to the location you choose and tells you that is what it is.

05

Sandbox and permissions

The app runs inside Apple's App Sandbox. It can access its own container and files and folders you choose using the open panel, drag and drop, Finder or Shortcuts. Those grants are stored on your Mac as security-scoped bookmarks so you aren't asked again on relaunch — Apple's mechanism, not ours, and not information sent to the developer.

06

Third parties

The app uses third-party libraries, including Realm, Lottie and ZIPFoundation. It does not use advertising or analytics services. Apple handles App Store transactions and may process information according to its own policies.

Local app data

The app stores preferences, recent file references, saved queries and other working data locally. You can clear recent files in the app and delete safety copies and snapshots in Settings. macOS and your backup or sync services may retain their own copies according to their settings.

07

Children's privacy

Data Inspector Pro is a developer tool, not directed at children, and collects no personal information from anyone who uses it.

08

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the date above will change with it. Given what the app does, the substance is unlikely to.
