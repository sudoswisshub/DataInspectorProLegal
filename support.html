Frequently asked

Why does the app ask for access to a whole folder?

Only for formats that keep sibling files alongside the one you opened — SQLite in WAL mode needs its -wal and -shm files, and Realm needs its .lock and .management files. The app explains exactly which files it needs before asking, and "Open Anyway" lets you decline and continue without them.

Is it safe to edit my database?

Files open read-only by default. The first time you unlock one for editing, a safety copy is made automatically, so File ▸ Revert to Safety Copy can put the original back exactly as it was. Every edit, insert and delete is also undoable with ⌘Z.

Which file formats are supported?

SQLite, Core Data and SwiftData (all three share SQLite's on-disk format), Realm — including encrypted .realm files — plus JSON, JSON Lines and property lists.

Does the app send my data anywhere?

No. There's no network code in the app at all. See the Privacy Policy for the full detail.

I found a bug or want a feature. Where do I report it?

Email alice.turcanu91@gmail.com with what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead. A screenshot and the file format involved (SQLite, Realm, Core Data, SwiftData, JSON, or plist) help the most.
